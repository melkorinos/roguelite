Overall risk: LOW. The full A–F batch is in: damage-accounting (HP-conservation invariant over a 24-board corpus + exact mitigation/curse arithmetic), every per-modifier field (B), timing as a fight consequence (D: haste/shock/freeze → fire counts), and side-isolation (E: no status leaks across sides). The batch caught three real bugs (two attribution, one gameplay — see below). Only one low-severity gap remains (lethal-fight overkill/heal accounting, deliberately outside the conservation domain), plus a few partials worth tightening. Known follow-up: the cosmetic haste status (its reduction field isn't read by the cooldown math) — a small design task, not a correctness hole.

What the invariant already caught (evidence it works)

Bug 1 — curse amplification double-counted on direct hits. _fire_element_once credited the curse amp to both contrib["direct"] (inside dmg) and contrib["curse"] → the Summary overstated curse-build damage. Witness: board 16, removed 21 but credited 24.
Bug 2 — deal_damage ability atom credited nothing. supernova's detonation / combat_start nukes removed HP but appeared in no Contribution bucket (invisible in Summary). Witness: board 20, removed 131 but credited 121 — the missing 10 = supernova's bonus.
Bug 3 — haste was inverted (GAMEPLAY, caught by batch D). Combat timers count UP to the cooldown, but the haste_timers atom SUBTRACTED from them → haste made a side fire slower, the opposite of its purpose. Compounding: the haste status's reduction field is display-only, so most haste abilities did nothing to fire rate. Fixed: haste_timers now advances timers (fires sooner).

All three fixed; suite green. Two were invisible to every isolated unit test — exactly the composition-bug class this batch exists to kill (original trigger: a poison element that dealt damage but reported 0).
