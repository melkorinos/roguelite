Fire — New Shapes
Bonfire Spread
Three tongues fanning outward from a common base — the outer two lean sideways, center rises tallest. Wide silhouette, nothing like a tail.
Helix Flame
The flame body twists as it rises — lower half leans left, upper half leans right. The S-curve makes it immediately distinctive and implies motion.
Crescent Flame
A crescent-ring of fire with a shard-tongue breaking through the gap. Circular silhouette is very distinct, and the inner spike adds asymmetry and menace.

Water — Drop + Wave Variants
Drop + Ripples
Teardrop floating above three concentric oval ripple rings. Implies the moment of impact. The rings give visual weight at the base without needing a wave line.
Drop + Crown
Droplet descending toward a crown-splash. The spikes frame the bottom half. More dynamic and aggressive than a calm wave — implies force and precision.
Drop + Breaking Wave
Classic drop above a curling wave with a proper breaking crest and foam. The most narrative — tells a whole story. Droplets flying off the crest add energy.
