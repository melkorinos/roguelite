Element Art Concepts

WoW / Pokemon hybrid — painterly-graphic, bold silhouettes, slight surreal wrongness

🔥 Fire — Burn
Coiling Flame
Three-lobe upward curl. Dark orange core→yellow tip. Thick outer stroke (Pokemon), soft painted gradient (WoW). Asymmetric — left lobe bigger.
Ember Burst
Central glowing sphere with radial spark rays. Implies burst / explosion damage. More dynamic, reads as "instant" rather than "ongoing".
Ancient Brand
Flame-shaped sigil / rune with a dashed outer ring. Uncanny artifact energy — surreal rather than natural. Fits the "weird" soul note best.
💧 Water — Cleanse
Drop + Wave
Classic teardrop with wave crest. Universal read. Risk: generic. Best if the overall icon set needs a clear "safe" anchor for water.
Spiral Whirlpool
Top-down vortex, dark eye at center. Unique silhouette. Implies pulling something out — matches the Cleanse mechanic. Stands opposite to Fire's vertical form.
Flowing Ribbon
S-curve of flowing water, translucent layered with foam. More painterly, elegant. Better as a larger piece of art — harder to read at small tile sizes.
Style Spectrum — Same Icon, Three Treatments

Fire coiling flame at three points on the WoW ↔ Pokemon axis. The middle column is the target zone.

Flat / Pokemon

Flat fill, bold outline, 2 colors only. High contrast. Reads at any size. Feels like a Pokémon type badge.

★ Target Zone

Thick outline + inner painted gradient. Slight glow. 3-color palette. Bold silhouette but with depth. This is the target.

Painterly / WoW

Thin or no outline, multi-stop gradient, soft outer glow. Very painted. Beautiful large, hard to read at 32px tile size.
Silhouette Test — All icons as filled black

Every icon must be distinguishable as a filled silhouette. If two look the same here, one needs a redesign.
