Design Notes
🧬 Damage Types & Status Effects Planned

Each element will carry a passive effect that triggers on hit or cooldown tick — layered on top of raw damage. The effect identity should feel inseparable from the element's nature.

🔥 Burn ☠️ Poison 💚 Heal 🐢 Slow 🌑 Blind ❄️ Freeze ⚡ Shock 🛡️ Shield
  • Does an effect replace or stack with raw damage?
  • Do higher-tier elements get stronger versions of the same effect, or a different one?
  • Should T3 results combine the effects of their two ingredient elements?
  • How do debuff effects interact with Ability Chain order — does blind skip the next element's hit?
  • Can two of the same effect stack (e.g. two Poison sources)?
🔓 Open-Ended Forging Exploring

Current model: forging is a fixed lookup table (RecipeData). The alternative treats forging as a core discovery mechanic — recipes are not pre-defined or fully visible; players experiment and uncover combinations themselves.

This shifts forging from "craft what I already know" to "figure out what exists" — which fits the break the system over dozens of sessions soul much more tightly. discovered_recipes[] already exists in PlayerProfile as a seam for this.

🧪 Discovery-driven 📖 Compendium unlock 🌐 Shared meta 🔀 Emergent combos
  • Are unknown recipes hidden entirely, or teased (silhouette / "???")?
  • Is discovery per-run or persistent across matches (PlayerProfile)?
  • Could the community collectively discover recipes — shared meta-layer?
  • What happens when you forge two elements with no recipe — random, failure, or "junk" element?
  • Does this mean more than the current 30 recipes, or the same 30 but hidden?
  • How does it interact with shop tier weights — do T3 recipes stay dark until a T2 pair is tried?
⚖️ Hidden Item Efficiency Function Internal Tool

A developer-only scoring function invisible to players. Assigns each element a total efficiency score by weighting all its properties — raw DPS, effect type, effect strength, and tier premium. Used to detect outliers and tune balance without exposing the math.

efficiency(e) =
  w_dps × (eff_dmg / cooldown)
  + w_effect × effect_value(e)
  + w_tier × tier_bonus(e)

// effect_value: Burn=0.8, Poison=1.2, Heal=1.5,
// Slow=1.0, Blind=1.3, Freeze=1.4, Shock=0.9 ...
// weights are tunable constants, not shown to players
  • Should efficiency be a single float or a multi-axis vector (offence vs utility vs support)?
  • Target score band per tier — e.g. T1: 3–5, T2: 6–9, T3: 10–14?
  • How are effects that scale with combo context valued (e.g. Poison + Slow synergy)?
  • Show scores in a dev-only Compendium view, or only as a CI lint check?
  • Should forged results be auto-scored at design time or runtime?
