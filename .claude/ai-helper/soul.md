# Soul — Game Identity

## Core fantasy
Build insane synergies, break the system, get punished by your exact counter, and slowly master the meta over dozens of sessions.

## Player feeling (when a run goes perfectly)
The Ability Chain fires in the right order. Everything interacts. The opponent never stood a chance — not because of luck, but because you engineered it over several sessions of learning. The satisfaction is earned.

## Player feeling (when a run goes wrong)
You ran headfirst into your exact counter. It was fair. You understood exactly why. You already know what to build differently next time.

## Progression promise
Every session teaches you something. Your theoretical maximum slowly rises. There is always a new broken combination to discover.

## Game structure (settled)
- 8 players per match, async PvP + shared PvE rounds
- Single Player HP bar; eliminated when HP reaches zero
- Shop phase: arrive at shop → buy pieces (gold), draft Items/Trinkets (free), Merge/Forge to upgrade, click Fight
- Combat phase: Ability Chain resolves — pieces trigger Abilities in sequence; player fires one Innate Ability at a chosen moment
- Replay: watch the chain, spend a token to replay it with Innate Ability timed differently
- Meta layer: light progression unlocking new Factions, Synergies, and milestones

## Optional direction (not committed)
PvE Bosses as damage/defence check milestones. Beating them unlocks new content that changes the meta permanently for that account.

## Aesthetic
Clean, minimal, weird, surreal. Not cute, not grimdark. Uncanny item energy with polished execution.

## Platform
Steam desktop. Async multiplayer. Sessions: 20–30 minutes.

## Inspirations
Super Auto Pets, Hearthstone Battlegrounds, The Bazaar, Dota Auto Chess — filtered through a meta-progression identity and ability-chain combat.

## Design north star
"Break the system with synergies you engineered over dozens of sessions."
