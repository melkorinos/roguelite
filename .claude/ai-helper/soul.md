# Soul — Game Identity

## Core fantasy
Engineer unstoppable synergies through scavenging and risk.

## Player feeling (when a run goes perfectly)
Puzzle satisfaction with a chaos payoff. The player deliberately engineers a synergy — the result is chaotic and surprising. The game must be learnable, not random. Outcomes should feel emergent.

## Run structure (tentative)
Extraction. Enter a zone with nothing, scavenge, build broken synergies from what you find, escape alive. Tension lives in the risk-vs-reward of staying longer.

## Inspirations
Vampire Survivors, Brotato, Risk of Rain 2, Slay the Spire — filtered through a scavenger identity and system-driven chaos.

## Design north star
"Broken tools that shouldn't work together but do."
